You can't click the cookie. You have to review. Cookie Recall turns your daily reviews into a cosy idle game. Every card you answer bakes cookies. Spend them on Grannies, wood ovens and windmills, all the way up to the Cosmic Prism, and watch your bakery grow at the pace of your learning. There is no passive income. Buildings never bake on their own: they multiply what each review is worth. Close Anki and nothing happens. Open it, clear your cards, and the whole bakery comes to life. 🍪 While you review • A small counter in the corner shows "+N" on every answer, your combo and your next purchase. • Combos: keep reviewing without long breaks and each answer is worth more. Consistency counts, never speed. • Golden cookies pop up next to the card. Catch one for Lucky, a ×7 Frenzy or a cookie rain. • Plain cards get a paper-card look, a friendlier font and coloured answer buttons. Cards with their own styling are left exactly as they are. ▶ From your decks straight into reviews A "Study now" card sits above your decks, and every deck gets its own ▶ Study button. Or just press S. No more detour through the overview screen. 🏪 A bakery that keeps growing • 14 buildings, 142 upgrades and 135 achievements, some of them secret. • Clear the oven: finish all your due cards for the day to win a big jackpot and extend your streak. A Protective Frosting saves it if you miss a day. • Knowledge: a permanent bonus that grows with your mature cards, so with what you have actually learned. • Three daily missions and Diplomas, a prestige layer with a Legacy tree of permanent upgrades. • Balanced to last for months, whether you do 50 or 500 reviews a day. 🎨 Make it yours Pick one of five flavours (Pistachio, Raspberry, Blueberry, Chocolate or Lavender) with the 🎨 button on the bakery's awning. The bakery, the review counter, your cards and an optional theme for every Anki window all follow along: deck list, Add Cards, Browser, Statistics, Deck Options and menus. 🌙 Light and dark mode Everything is designed for both, and follows Anki's own setting. ✅ Built to help your learning, not hurt it • Every answer earns the same. "Again" earns as much as "Easy", so lying to the scheduler never pays. • Rewards are reduced for the same card seen again on the same day, for answers faster than a second, and beyond 50 new cards a day (you can change these limits). • The biggest rewards come from the habits that matter: finishing your due cards every day and growing mature cards. ⚙️ Settings Open the bakery's Stats tab, or go to Tools → Add-ons → Cookie Recall → Config. You can change the colour palette, the card style, the language (English or French), the Anki theme, sounds, the counter's position and reduced motion. Good to know • Works with Anki Desktop 23.10 and newer (tested on 26.9). • Your progress is saved in your collection and syncs through AnkiWeb between computers. • Add-ons don't run on AnkiMobile or AnkiDroid, so reviews done on your phone don't bake cookies. • Cookie Recall never changes your cards, note types or scheduling, and everything can be switched off in one click.
