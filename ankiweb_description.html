🍪 Cookie Recall — the Cookie Clicker that only bakes when you review English & French. (Version française plus bas.) You can't click the cookie: every review bakes cookies. Buy Grannies, Wood ovens, Windmills… all the way up to the Cosmic prism, and watch your bakery grow at the pace of your learning. Everything stays inside Anki, no separate window: • While reviewing: a small counter in a corner, with "+N" on every answer, your combo, your next purchase and the golden cookie to catch. • On the deck list: the full bakery, with the display shelves, the shop, daily missions, achievements, diplomas and stats. • On a deck's page: a one-line strip showing your cookies. • Cookie Recall theme: re-skins all of Anki in the same universe (top bar, deck list, Add cards, Browser, Statistics, Deck options, menus), in light and dark mode. It never touches your card content, and you can turn it off in one click. No passive income Buildings don't produce anything on their own: they raise the value of each review. If you don't review, nothing happens. What keeps you coming back • Combo: reviews chained without a long break earn more and more. Consistency counts, never speed. • Golden cookie: pops up at random while you review. Catch it for Lucky, a ×7 Frenzy or a cookie rain. • Clear the oven: finish all your due cards for the day for a big jackpot and +1 streak day. • Streak: every successful day boosts your earnings. A Protective frosting, bought with cookies, saves it if you miss a day. • Knowledge: a bonus that grows with your mature cards, so with what you've truly learned. • 14 buildings, ~140 upgrades, 135 achievements (some secret), 3 missions a day. • Diplomas: the prestige layer. Start over in exchange for permanent bonuses, spent in the Legacy tree. Built not to hurt your learning • Every answer earns the same, "Again" as much as "Easy". Lying to the scheduler never pays. • Reduced rewards for the same card reviewed several times in a day, for answers under one second, and beyond 50 new cards a day (configurable). • The biggest rewards come from what really matters: finishing your due cards every day and growing mature cards. Settings In the bakery (Stats tab) or under Tools → Add-ons → Cookie Recall → Config: language, Cookie Recall theme, sounds, counter during reviews and its position, reduced motion, collapsed bakery. Compatibility • Anki Desktop 23.10 or newer (tested on 26.9), light and dark themes. • Your game is saved in your collection and syncs through AnkiWeb between computers. • Add-ons don't run on AnkiMobile or AnkiDroid: reviews done on mobile don't bake cookies. • Doesn't change your cards, note types or Anki's scheduling.
